Privacy, in plain words

  • Every tool is one self-contained web page. There's no server behind it, no cookies, and no analytics.
  • What you type stays in your browser tab. Close the tab and it's gone.
  • Links to official websites (like FTC, HealthCare.gov, or Google Help) open those sites. Their own privacy policies apply there.
  • You can save any tool to your device (in your browser, choose Save page as…) and use it offline.
Important: These tools give general information and templates, with links to official sources. They are not legal, medical, tax, or financial advice, and they aren't affiliated with any government agency or company.
